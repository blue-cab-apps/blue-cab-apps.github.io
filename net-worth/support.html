Using the app

Does Net Worth connect to my bank?

No. You type in your balances yourself, and the app never connects to a bank or any other financial service. Your net worth is everything you own minus everything you owe.

How do balances and readings work?

Add each thing you own or owe in the Accounts tab, then open an account whenever you want to enter its current balance. Each account shows when you last updated it, and it’s highlighted once that’s been a while.

Each day you change a balance, the app saves a reading of that day’s totals. There is one reading per day: a later update on the same day replaces that day’s reading. Your readings make up the line in History and the changes you see on the Dashboard.

What is a past estimate?

A rough figure for what your net worth was in an earlier month, so your history doesn’t have to start today. Setup offers to take one, and you can add more at any time from History → Add a past estimate: pick the month and enter roughly where you stood.

An estimate appears as a hollow point on your chart. It counts toward your line and your personal best, but not toward pace or projections, and it carries no account or category detail. A month that already holds a real reading can’t be given an estimate.

What does Hide Balances do?

It blurs the amounts on the Dashboard, for moments when someone might be looking over your shoulder. Turn it on or off with the eye button on the Dashboard or in Settings → Display. It stays the way you left it. It only changes what’s shown on screen and isn’t a security feature.

Premium

What’s in Premium?

The free version keeps one account per category and one net-worth goal with a suggested target. Premium adds more accounts per category, your own goal targets, and goals for a category or a single account. It costs $2.99 per month or $34.99 per year (US prices; the App Store shows your local price before you confirm).

What does “2 months of Premium included” mean?

Every new user gets two months of Premium features at no cost. They start when the app tells you about them, after setup. Nothing is charged, no payment details are asked for, and nothing renews. Settings → Premium shows the date they end, and if you allow notifications, the app reminds you three days before.

You can subscribe at any time, including during the included months. Subscribing during them starts billing straight away.

How do I change or cancel my subscription?

  • In the app: tap the gear at the top of the Dashboard to open Settings, then Premium → Manage Subscription.
  • In iOS: open the Settings app → [your name] → Subscriptions → Net Worth.

Apple handles all billing. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. When you cancel, Premium stays on until the end of the period you’ve already paid for. For refunds, contact Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have a new iPhone, or I reinstalled the app. How do I get Premium back?

Open Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases (it’s also on the Premium screen). It finds a subscription bought with the same Apple Account.

What happens if Premium ends?

Nothing is deleted, merged or changed. Accounts beyond the free limit stay visible, still count in your net worth, and are marked with a lock and “Premium”. You can still delete them if you want to. Goals with your own target, and goals for a category or an account, are kept but locked in the same way. Subscribing again unlocks everything exactly as you left it.

Your data

How do I erase everything?

Open Settings → Your data → Erase All Data…. This deletes every account, reading, estimate and goal from your iPhone and from iCloud, so it also disappears from your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. It can’t be undone. Erasing doesn’t cancel a subscription; manage that under Manage Subscription.

To remove Net Worth’s iCloud data entirely, use iOS itself: Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (the name varies slightly between iOS versions) → Net Worth.

If I delete the app, is my data gone?

If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, your data is still in your iCloud account, and it comes back when you reinstall Net Worth with the same Apple Account. If you weren’t signed in to iCloud, your data was only on that iPhone, and deleting the app deletes it.

iCloud

How does iCloud sync work?

There’s nothing to set up and no Net Worth account to create. If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, Net Worth automatically keeps a copy of your data in your own private iCloud storage, and any other iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account shows the same data. Apple stores it for you; we can’t see it.

The line under your net worth on the Dashboard shows where things stand, for example “Synced with iCloud”, “Syncing with iCloud…” or “Offline — changes will sync to iCloud later”.

What does “Not signed in to iCloud — saved on this iPhone only” mean?

Your iPhone isn’t signed in to iCloud, so Net Worth is saving your data on this iPhone only. Everything in the app still works. The difference is that there’s no copy in iCloud: your data won’t appear on your other devices, and if you lose this iPhone or delete the app, your data goes with it.

To fix it, open the Settings app and sign in with your Apple Account at the top. If the message is still there afterwards, check that Net Worth is allowed to use iCloud in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. You don’t need to re-enter anything.

You may also see:

  • “iCloud is restricted on this device”: iCloud is limited by Screen Time or by whoever manages the device. Your data is saved on this iPhone.
  • “iCloud sync didn’t finish — your data is safe on this iPhone”: the last sync attempt didn’t complete. Your data is still on your iPhone, and syncing tries again automatically.

Privacy

Can you see my finances?

No. Your data stays on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud storage, and it never reaches us. There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The details are in the Net Worth Privacy Policy.
