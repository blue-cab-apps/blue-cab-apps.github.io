Net Worth

Know your net worth, up or down.

One number for everything you own minus everything you owe, and how it moves over time. You type the balances yourself. Nothing to sign up for.

You type the balances

Add what you own: cash, investments, property, a vehicle, retirement savings, anything else with a balance. Add what you owe: cards, loans, a mortgage. Open an account whenever you want to enter its current balance. Each day you change one, the app saves that day’s totals as an update, and every update becomes a point on your line.

No bank logins, no spending categories, no transactions. One currency, shown with the $ sign.

Private by design

There is no account and nothing to sign up for. What you enter stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. Apple hosts that storage and we have no access to it.

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code. The app has no server of its own and sends nothing to us. The App Store privacy label says the same: Data Not Collected.

Honest numbers

A down month shows as down. A new account is not growth. Every forecast says its pace: “~June 2027 at your 3-month pace”. A past estimate is marked as an estimate, so your first chart can already show change without pretending to know more than you told it.

Reminders on your day

An update reminder on the day and at the time you choose: weekly, every two weeks, monthly, or on payday. Hide balances with one tap when someone is looking over your shoulder.

Free, with two months of Premium included

The free version keeps one account per category and one net-worth goal with a suggested target. Premium adds more accounts per category, your own goal targets, and goals for a category or a single account. It costs $2.99 per month or $34.99 per year (US prices; the App Store shows your local price before you confirm).

Every new user gets two months of Premium features at no cost. They start when the app tells you about them, after setup. Nothing is charged, no payment details are asked for, and nothing renews. When they end, nothing is deleted, merged or changed.
