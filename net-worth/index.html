A calm way to keep score.

No feeds, no budgets, no transactions. One number for everything you own minus everything you owe, and how it moves.

The monthly check-in.

A reminder on the day you choose opens the check-in. Each account in turn: type the new balance, or tap Keep if nothing changed.

One account at a time

You see every account on Ready to save before anything is saved. Stop halfway and it is kept on your iPhone, with one reminder to pick it up if you use reminders.

A recap of the month

What moved, your pace, and milestones. A down month shows as down. A new account is not counted as growth.

Goals, one milestone at a time

Reach one target, then the app suggests the next. Forecasts always say their pace.

Everything you own and owe

Cash, investments, CPF, property, vehicles, cards, loans and mortgages. One currency, shown with the $ sign.

Keep it private.

There is no account and nothing to sign up for. You type your balances. They stay on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. Apple hosts that storage and we have no access to it.

No bank logins

Nothing connects to your bank, and the app never moves money.

Nothing shared

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code. The app has no server of its own and sends nothing to us.
